A business analytics presentation exploring BMW sales performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), model-level sales, regional revenue, fuel-type mix, yearly trends, and customer segments. The dashboard is designed to help readers compare performance, identify patterns, and highlight areas that may require further investigation.
📌 Project Overview
This project presents a structured walkthrough of a BMW Sales Dashboard, explaining the purpose of its KPI cards, slicer, charts, and the business questions each view can help answer.
The presentation covers overall performance, product/model performance, geographic contribution, fuel-type distribution, changes over time, and customer-type mix. It concludes with key business insights, recommendations, and the role of dashboard reporting in supporting decisions.

🔍 Key Observations Highlighted in the Presentation
Model performance: Sales volumes differ across models; the strongest and weaker performers merit separate attention.
Regional contribution: Regional revenue comparisons can help identify stronger markets and potential growth opportunities.
Fuel mix: Sales are not evenly distributed across fuel categories; one category contributes the largest share.
Time-based fuel trends: Comparing fuel types over multiple years can reveal changes in customer demand.
Revenue movement: The presentation flags a 2024 revenue peak and a 2025 decline for validation and further investigation.
Customer segments: The sales mix depends heavily on one customer segment, making retention and diversification relevant considerations.
Important: These are qualitative observations stated in the presentation. Numerical values, causes, and the significance of any changes should be validated against the underlying dataset before making business decisions.
💡 Business Recommendations
Optimize model-level decisions: Review inventory and promotional plans using model-level sales performance.
Investigate regional differences: Study high- and low-contribution regions before setting market-specific sales actions.
Monitor fuel-type demand: Track changes in the fuel mix and assess whether product and marketing plans align with observed demand.
Validate revenue trends: Confirm the latest reporting period and investigate the noted 2024 peak and 2025 decline.
Strengthen customer relationships: Understand the needs of the largest customer segment while exploring opportunities to grow other segments.
Use slicers for focused analysis: Filter by model or series to make comparisons more targeted and actionable.
